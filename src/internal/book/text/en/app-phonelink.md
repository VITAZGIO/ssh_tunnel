# Phone link

Your phone and computers as one. Add the plugin **on both** devices; it works
over the device network.

- **Find device** — the phone plays your track (or the alarm), rising to full volume in 3 s even when silent. Stop: turn the phone screen on, «Stop» in the notification or **■ Stop** on another device; it stops by itself after a minute. Pick the track in this plugin on the phone.
- **Music and volume** on the chosen device.
- **🖼 Photo** — pick a photo on the phone; the PC puts it into the clipboard and pastes it (Ctrl+V).
- **Touchpad and keyboard (for a PC)** — move, tap to click, two fingers to
  scroll; type text on the PC. Linux needs xdotool.
- **Notifications** from the phone appear on the PC (list and tray popup).
  On the phone grant notification access.
