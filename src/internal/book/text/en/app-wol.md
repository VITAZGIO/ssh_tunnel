# Wake on LAN

Turn a computer on with a button. The program broadcasts a «magic packet» to
the local network and the network card of the sleeping machine wakes it.

1. Enter the name and MAC — «Add». The MAC is shown by the Network scanner on
   a computer or in the router.
2. «Wake» — the machine wakes in 10–60 seconds.

It needs the same local network, Wake-on-LAN on in BIOS/UEFI and in the
network card settings, and usually a cable: most machines do not wake over
Wi-Fi.
