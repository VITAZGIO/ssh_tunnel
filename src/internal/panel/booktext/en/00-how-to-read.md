# How to read this Book

This is the help for the server panel. It lives inside the panel itself and works offline: the text is built into the program, it is not loaded from a website.

The Book is about the **panel**. The program on a device (the “Through the server” and “Device network” buttons, the window, the apps) has its own Book — open it inside that program.

## Where it is

“Settings” → “Book”. On a wide screen the list of chapters is on the left and the text on the right. On a phone you see the list first, then the chosen chapter full-screen; the “Back” button returns to the list.

## How to search

1. Type a word into the “Search the Book” field above the chapter list.
2. Below it you get the places where it occurs: the chapter title and the line with the word.
3. Tap a line — the chapter opens at that place with the word highlighted.

Search covers all chapters in the chosen language. The Book follows the panel language: switch the interface language and the Book switches too.

## Opening a chapter straight from the panel

Section titles have a “?” mark next to them. If a section has its own chapter, tap the “?” — the pop-up hint shows a “More in the Book” link. No chapter — no link.

## Where you stopped

The panel remembers which chapter you were reading and where. After a page refresh the Book opens at the same place. It is remembered in this browser only.

## If there are few chapters

The Book is written in parts, so some chapters may appear later. The list shows exactly the chapters that exist in the installed version of the panel. New chapters arrive with a panel update: “Settings” → “Check for updates”.
